Setting & story.

A kingdom in pieces

The Lands Between stand broken, fractured by the shattering of the Elden Ring and the catastrophic war known as the Shattering that followed. Governed by Queen Marika the Eternal, this vast world was once blessed by the Golden Order and the giant Erdtree whose leaves showered grace upon the land. Now, demigod children—each claiming a fragment of the shattered Great Runes—fight over the remnants of power, driven mad by their newfound strength. As a Tarnished, a soul once exiled from grace and called back by destiny, you awaken in a graveyard of fallen champions to claim the title of Elden Lord.

A choice beyond the throne

Traversing the shimmering golden plains of Limgrave to the rotting red wastes of Caelid and the sunken academies of Liurnia, the journey is an intricate tapestry of cosmic gods, tragic betrayals, and forgotten lineages. Guided by the enigmatic Maiden Melina and tested by legendary foes like Malenia, Blade of Miquella, and Starscourge Radahn, your choices will ultimately determine the future of the Lands Between. Will you restore the shattered Golden Order, usher in a new age of stars, or burn the world away in the chaotic flames of the Frenzied Flame?
